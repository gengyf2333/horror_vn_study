“Phil!” says the master, walking towards him without his coat and waistcoat, and looking more soldierly than ever in his braces. “You were found in a doorway, weren’t you?” “Gutter,” says Phil. “Watchman tumbled over me.” “Then vagabondizing came natural to YOU from the beginning.” “As nat’ral as possible,” says Phil. “Good night!” “Good night, guv’ner.” Phil cannot even go straight to bed, but finds it necessary to shoulder round two sides of the gallery and then tack off at his mattress. The trooper, after taking a turn or two in the rifle-distance and looking up at the moon now shining through the skylights, strides to his own mattress by a shorter route and goes to bed too.
